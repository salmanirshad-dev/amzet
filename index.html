About the school

A public school at the heart of Sopore.

01 — Introduction

Strengthening the academic foundation of the Zaingair belt.

Amzet Hall Public School sits at Malmapanpora, Zaingair, in Sopore — the “Apple Town of Kashmir” in Baramulla district. Under the chairmanship of Dr. Abrar Ahmad, the school takes part in district-level initiatives and serves families across the surrounding area.

03 — Gallery
From the ground

Students, staff and the wider community.

In May 2026, Amzet Hall Public School collaborated with the Zonal Physical Education Zone Dangerpora to organise a Cross Country Run under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan. The run began at Botingoo Sopore and concluded at the school in Malmapanpora.

Dr. Abrar Ahmad, Chairman, attended alongside ZPEO Shabir Ali and other dignitaries — reflecting the school's active role in district-level social initiatives.
